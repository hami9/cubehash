# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Contributors and maintainers of **CubeHash** pledge to make participation in this project and community a harassment-free, respectful, and technically constructive experience for everyone.

## Standards

- Focus reviews and discussions on cryptographic correctness, verifiable benchmarks, reproducible tests, and constructive engineering feedback.
- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or disclosing private security reports publicly prior to coordinated disclosure are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the repository maintainer (`@hami9`).
